Selected work

  • ZEST: Zero-shot Embodied Skill Transfer
    Co-author · 2026

    A framework for transferring manipulation skills to new robot embodiments and tasks without per-target fine-tuning. Policies trained in simulation generalize zero-shot to unseen hardware and object configurations.

    Video
  • MSc thesis — Target Object Search using Interactive Perception
    ETH Zurich, Robotic Systems Lab · 2023

    TOSIP retrieves a language-specified object from a cluttered tabletop using a fixed 4-DoF arm and overhead camera, combining Double Q-Learning over push/grasp actions with an Open Vocabulary Fusion layer (CLIPSeg + geometric features) for free-form text prompts. Outperforms the VPG baseline at 95.1% grasp success, on a 17× faster NVIDIA Omniverse reimplementation of the environment.

    Videos: open-vocabulary search · object interaction
  • Rowesys — Rosie, an autonomous weeding robot
    ETH Zurich, Focus Project (team of 11) · 2020

    Nine-month Bachelor capstone: the Rowesys team designed and built Rosie, a four-wheel-drive field robot for organic sugar-beet farming. Cameras drive autonomous row-following and weed detection; mechanical goosefoot sweepers remove weeds between rows — no herbicides.

    Video
  • Image-Based Weight Estimation for Waste Items
    ETH Zurich, Robotic Systems Lab · 2022

    Semester thesis comparing class-average, regression, and neural-network approaches for estimating the mass of individual waste items from images. 2D regression won, predicting 96.4% of items within a 0.5×–2× tolerance band on a new dataset of 325 items across seven waste categories.
